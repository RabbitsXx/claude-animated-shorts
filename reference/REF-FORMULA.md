## What makes it fun (rules we gate)
1. One fact (max 2), said once, with a picture the viewer already knows; applied three times afterwards (plant, hero, rival).
2. One carrier object in about half the beats and it changes shape (circle -> hammered -> tall and skinny).
3. Hero is stubborn and looks wrong; rival is smug and loses to the same rule; the hero's blunder and the rival's sabotage both end up helping the hero.
4. Narration: But/So chain, 10-12 words per sentence, about 227 words per minute, almost no numbers, repeats the names of what is on screen, one rhetorical question at the lowest-stakes moment.
5. Light: bright day -> night (secret, tense work) -> bright morning (public); luminance swing above 0.2; about 30% night.
6. Stakes as in-world text (banner "OPENING TOMORROW", "GRAND OPENING", road sign), captions only 1-3 words per pop at 0.69 of frame height.
7. Editing: about 5 locations, about 6 s per shot, dissolves between sections, cuts inside them; camera always travels; acting in every shot (eyes, brows, mouth, head, hands on separate timings); setup -> contact -> consequence for every spoken action.
8. Sound: voice first, a low corporate-style bed with one style change, a riser at the main cut, whip-pan whooshes and a ding at the payoff, -14 LUFS.
