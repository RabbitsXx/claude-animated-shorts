# Hook for the first 3 seconds

Reference pattern (`reference/REF-FORMULA.md`): a person doing something odd in the first two seconds, consequence withheld, the stake in line 1. Not a question, not a number, not "what if".

## Three alternative opening lines
1. "Pete just hung a giant golden ball inside his skyscraper." (9 words, about 3 s) - an odd act, the reason withheld, the laughter promised.
2. "Why would anyone hang a ball in a skyscraper?" - the title already asks it, and nothing is shown.
3. "This tower should not stand still." - vague, no person.

Chosen: line 1.

## Shot list for 0-3.2 s
Frame 1 is already moving: a golden ball the size of a bus is mid-air on a crane hook, Pete at the lever below, the skinny tower behind.
