# Time Machine - beat map copied from the reference skeleton

Only the topic changes. Each row keeps the job of the reference beat (see `reference/study/ANALYSIS-TH.md` section 8). Times are targets for a ~60 s cut; the real times come from aligned voice words.

World: one town-fair field. Inventor's open-air workshop (left), rival's sponsored platform (right), fairground and stage (far), model railway/orrery table inside the workshop for the explainer. Day, with one dusk/lamp-lit shot for the first test.

## Acceptance notes specific to this film
- Characters: real 3D head and torso with 2D facial ink and stick limbs (user instruction). Inventor and rival must differ by costume, hair, posture; crowd must have varied heights/costumes.
- All numbers on in-world signs/screens are in-world 3D text (allowed), not captions.
- No beat may be a held pose; every beat has anticipation -> contact -> consequence.
